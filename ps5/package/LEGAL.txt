WHAT IS LEFT OUT, AND WHY. PCSX2's collection of game patches (patches.zip: widescreen,
no-interlacing, 60 fps) is not in this package: its repository states no licence. The title
fetches it from that repository's releases when you ask it to, for your own use (Settings >
Interface > "Download PCSX2's patches"), or you copy it in yourself: README.md, "Game patches".
No cover pictures, title screens or pictures of games are in this package either: the title
fetches them for your own use from libretro's collection of thumbnails
(github.com/libretro-thumbnails), by a game's name, after it has looked for games, unless you
switch that off (Settings > Interface). They are other people's work and stay on your console.

THE NAME, THE MARK AND THE START-UP SOUND. "Crux2", its mark (one broad bar crossing two thin
ones) and the pictures made of them are this port's own; the letters are Saira ExtraBold's (SIL
Open Font License 1.1). The start-up sound was supplied by me for this title, and it is mine to
pass on with it. Before v0.6.0 the title was named PSPCSX2.

MEMORY CARDS AND SAVE FILES. The memory card manager reads and writes PlayStation 2 memory card
files and reads save files of several kinds (.psu, .max, .cbs, .sps, .xps, .psv). How they are
laid out it knows from the source of mymc+ (Florian Maerkl and contributors, based on mymc by
Ross Ridge; GPL-3.0-or-later), after which that part was written anew; the unpacking of .max
files is after Haruhiko Okumura's LZARI. No save is in this package. Saves are data of the games
they belong to: bring in your own, or ones their makers let you have.

ONLINE PLAY. The first name server of the PlayStation 2's network adapter is set to that of
PS2 Online (ps2online.com), a service run by other people that keeps some games' online play
going. Crux2 has no part in that service and sends it nothing itself: with the adapter switched
on, a game asks that server for addresses, as it would on a PlayStation 2 set up for it.
Settings > Network changes or removes it.
