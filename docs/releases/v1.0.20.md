# Crux2 v1.0.20

> [!IMPORTANT]
> No games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are included, and none ever will be. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and a BIOS dumped from your own PlayStation 2.

**Not yet run on a console.** Everything in this release was built and checked on a PC only (the host tests, the interface's automatic walk-through and its screenshots). Please try it and report what you find.

## Added
- **One anti-aliasing row** (Settings > Graphics > Post-processing): Off, FXAA, SMAA, SSAA or SSAA + SMAA. SSAA draws the game larger than the screen and then reduces it; SSAA + SMAA adds SMAA on top.
- **HD texture pack manager**, from the library's menu, Settings > Graphics, and a game's page: every pack on the console, the USB drives and the shares, by game, with its size. Move a pack between places (copied and checked before the original goes), delete it, or download a collection's packs for your games.
- **Performance overlay in three sizes** (Settings > On-Screen Display, Performance Overlay Size): Minimal (the frame rate), Compact (frame rate, GPU, EE, GS and VU) and Full (frame times, a graph, 1% and 0.1% lows, loads, resolution, temperature and memory), and Custom, which shows what the switches under Show give.
- **A Home screen**, the first after the start-up: the game you played last, with Resume and its game page; what is going on right now (texture pack downloads, a disc in a drive, the share, the USB drives); and the games played lately.
- **A game's page**: the cover with Resume and Start from the beginning, and tabs for Overview, Save states, Its settings, Patches and cheats, and Texture packs.
- **Search** through every setting, at every level, and the library's games.
- **Pinned settings**: press Square on a setting to pin it; the pins show in Settings and in the quick menu, where they can be changed in place.

## Changed
- **The interface is laid out anew.** The top bar has Home, Library and Disc (while a disc is in a drive). Options opens the settings from any screen.
- **The library's places are a filter row** under the top bar: All, Console, USB, Share, then Favourites, Never played and Collections. All ten views are kept.
- **Settings are in eight areas**: Picture, Sound, Controls, Games and saves, Emulation, Network, Interface and System. Each area says what is set and how many settings are changed. The three levels are named Essentials, More and Everything. Every option of v1.0.19 is still there.
- **Every setting's description is taken word for word from PCSX2** where PCSX2 has the setting, and written in PCSX2's style where it does not.
- **The quick menu** has large tiles for Resume, Save state, Load state, Screenshot (the game's picture without the menu), Change disc and Quit game; your pinned settings; and More, with every other entry.
- **Motion** is shorter. With Motion set to Less, screens only fade.
- **The button hints** are in one order on every screen.
- **Messages** appear in the top right corner and never cover the hints.

## Fixed
- **Black screen with a Look** (Settings > Graphics > Look): the Look's picture is set up for the pass that reads it, and PCSX2's vertex buffer is bound again after it.
- **Touch pad**: a click on its left half could be taken for Start (right half for Select) when another finger was also on the pad. The finger that touched last decides now.
- **Corners** on skins with rounder or squarer corners were doubled; they are right now.

## Tested
On a PC only: the host tests, the interface's walk-through of every screen, and its screenshots in all 36 skins. Not yet run on a console: any of this release. In particular, Change disc between a disc image and a real drive, the quick menu's Screenshot, pinned settings written to a game's own settings, the performance overlay with PAL games, the Motion settings, and how quickly Home and Settings open with a large library.

## Installation
- Copy the `PPSA99249` folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, over an older one if you have it.
- Your games, BIOS files, memory cards and settings are kept. PCSX2 is the same revision as in v1.0.19, so save states from v1.0.19 should load; this is not yet checked on a console. Shaders are compiled again once after the update.
- Read `LEGAL.txt` and `README.md` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
- Open an issue and attach `crux2-boot.log` from the title folder (written after a crash, a hang or Save the log). Don't attach games, BIOS files, firmware or keys.
