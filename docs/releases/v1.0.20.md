# Crux2 v1.0.20

> [!IMPORTANT]
> No games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are included, and none ever will be. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and a BIOS dumped from your own PlayStation 2.

> [!WARNING]
> Save states made with v1.0.19 or earlier do not load in this version (PCSX2 changed their format). Save in the game, on a memory card, before you update; memory cards are kept.

## Added
- **PS1 Analog Controller** (DualShock 1) as a controller type, from PCSX2.
- **SMAA** is a switch beside FXAA (Settings > Picture > Post-processing) instead of an entry of the Look list. It runs before a look, so the two can be used together.
- A look, or SMAA, that draws only black on the console is switched off with a message.
- **USB headset** for games with voice chat (Settings > Controllers > USB microphones): player 1's microphone, and the earpiece on player 1's headset.

## Changed
- Built on PCSX2 v2.9.162 (was v2.9.158). Downloaded covers are named by serial and CRC; covers you have are still found.
- The graphics driver is on Mesa's development branch of 8 November (27.0-devel): shader compiler and binning fixes for the console's GPU.
- The pipelines a game needed last time are made on a thread of their own: no stalls in a game's first frames.
- Less work in the EE's floating point, with accurate floating point on or off.
- When frame generation shows the same picture twice, its look and side picture are made once.
- The side picture no longer builds frame generation's pipelines.

## Fixed
- Every look, and SMAA, left the screen black with no overlay.
- Accurate floating point on the VUs and VU0 used the game's own rounding fix instead of the PS2's rounding, in games that have one.
- The EE interpreter compared some floats (infinities, NaNs) differently from the recompiler.
- Accurate floating point's square root cleared the FPU's overflow and underflow flags.
- Paused or under a menu, an old picture enlarged by FSR could be shown instead of the game's own.

## Installation
- Copy the `PPSA99249` folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, over an older one if you have it.
- Your games, BIOS files, memory cards and settings are kept; save states from older versions are kept but do not load. Shaders are compiled again once after the update.
- Read `LEGAL.txt` and `README.md` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
- Open an issue and attach `crux2-boot.log` from the title folder, copied before Crux2 is started again. Don't attach games, BIOS files, firmware or keys.
