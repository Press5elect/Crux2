# Crux2 v1.0.20

> [!IMPORTANT]
> No games, BIOS files, firmware or keys are included, and none ever will be. Use only legally obtained backups of games you own and a BIOS dumped from your own PlayStation 2.

**Not yet run on a console.** This release was built and checked on a PC only (see Tested). Please try it and report what you find.

## Added
- **A Home screen**, the first after the start-up: the game you played last, with Resume and its game page; what is going on right now (texture pack downloads, a disc in a drive, the share, the USB drives, the console's games); and the games played lately.
- **A game's page** (Triangle on a game): the cover with Resume and Start from the beginning, and tabs (L1 / R1) for Overview, Save states, Its settings, Patches and cheats, and Texture packs.
- **Search** (Square on the settings' home) through every setting, at every level, and the library's games.
- **Pinned settings**: Square on a setting pins it. The pins show on the settings' home and in the quick menu, where they are changed in place.
- **One Anti-Aliasing row** (Settings > Picture > Graphics): Off, FXAA, SMAA, SSAA or SSAA + SMAA. SSAA draws the game larger than the screen and then reduces it, with a Soft or Sharp SSAA Filter; SSAA + SMAA adds SMAA on top.
- **HD texture pack manager** (Settings > Games and saves > Texture packs, the library's Square menu, and a game's page): every pack on the console, the USB drives and the shares, by game, with its size. Move a pack between places (copied and checked before the original goes), delete it, or download the collection's packs for your games one after another.
- **Performance overlay in three sizes** (Settings > Picture > On-Screen Display, Performance Overlay Size): Minimal (the frame rate), Compact (frame rate, GPU, EE, GS and VU) and Full (frame time, speed, 1% and 0.1% lows, a frame-time graph, the loads as bars, resolution, temperature and memory), and Custom, which shows what the switches under Show give. Amber means close to a limit, red over it. The FPS switch turns the whole overlay on and off.
- **A Screenshot tile** in the quick menu (the game's picture, without the menu).

## Changed
- **The interface is laid out anew.** The top bar has Home, Library and Disc (while a disc is in a drive), switched with L1 / R1. Options opens the settings from any screen, as before.
- **Console, USB and Share are no longer tabs**: they are a filter row at the top of the library (Up from the first row of games), with All, Favourites, Never played and Collections. All ten views are kept; views that did not show the focused game's details have a strip with them at the bottom.
- **Settings are in eight areas**: Picture, Sound, Controls, Games and saves, Emulation, Network, Interface and System. Each area's card says what is set and how many settings are changed; the home also shows the settings changed lately. The three levels are named Essentials, More and Everything (they were Normal, Advanced and Expert). **Every option of v1.0.19 is still there**, some under a new name or in a new place: search finds them.
- **Every setting's name and description is PCSX2's own, word for word**, where PCSX2 has the setting, and written in the same style where it does not.
- **A game's own settings**: L3 on a setting uses the global setting again; the touch pad switches to the settings of all games and back.
- **On a game's page, Square is More**: A favourite, Hide it, the next disc and Make an image of the disc are there now (they were L1, R1, Square and a button). L2 / R2 show the game's other pictures.
- **The quick menu** has large tiles for Resume, Save state, Load state, Screenshot, Change disc and Quit game; your pinned settings; and More, with every other entry (Triangle chooses and orders them, as before).
- **Motion** is shorter. With Motion set to Less, screens only fade.
- **The button hints** are in one order on every screen, and **messages** appear in the top right corner, never over the hints.

## Fixed
- **Black screen with a look** (Settings > Picture > Looks): the look's picture is set up for the pass that reads it, and PCSX2's vertex buffer is bound again after it.
- **Touch pad**: a click on its left half could be taken for Start (and the right half for Select) when another finger was also on the pad. The finger that touched last decides now.
- **Corners** on skins with rounder or squarer corners were rounded twice in places.

## Tested
On a PC only: the host tests, the interface's walk-through of every screen, the check that no setting was lost, and pictures of every new screen in all 36 skins. **Not yet run on a console: any of this release.** In particular: Resume from Home, Change disc between a disc image and a real drive, the quick menu's Screenshot, pinned settings written to a game's own settings, SSAA, the texture pack manager's move and delete, the performance overlay (with PAL games too), and how quickly Home and the settings open with a large library.

## Installation
- Copy the `PPSA99249` folder to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, over an older one if you have it.
- Your games, BIOS files, memory cards and settings are kept. PCSX2 is the same revision as in v1.0.19, so save states from v1.0.19 should load; this is not yet checked on a console. Shaders are compiled again once after the update.
- Read `LEGAL.txt` and `README.md` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
- Open an issue and attach `crux2-boot.log` from the title folder (written after a crash, a hang or Save the log). Don't attach games, BIOS files, firmware or keys.
